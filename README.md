# 🐛 BeanWorm

![Android Kotlin](https://img.shields.io/badge/language-android_kotlin-purple)

**Bean Worm** is an Android game focused on feeding, collecting, and interacting with adorable beanworms in a breeding box environment.

---

## 🎮 Game Overview

Bean Worm is a lighthearted and relaxing Android game where players take care of small creatures called **Bean Worms**.  
By feeding them, interacting with the environment, and collecting resources, players gradually build a lively and rewarding breeding space.

The game emphasizes **simple actions with meaningful feedback**, making it perfect for casual play sessions.

---

## ✨ Core Gameplay Fun

### 🐛 Feed the Beanworms  
Players feed food to beanworms to keep them active and healthy.  
Feeding affects their behavior and productivity, encouraging observation and gentle strategy.

### 💎 Collect Energy Ores  
Beanworms generate or attract **Energy Ores**, which serve as essential in-game resources.  
Players can manually collect these ores to unlock interactions, props, or improvements.

### 🧰 Use Props to Protect  
Various props can be used to:
- Protect beanworms  
- Improve the breeding environment  
- Handle unexpected situations  

This adds depth and variation to the care process.

### 🌱 Environment & Worm Interaction  
The game goes beyond idle mechanics:
- Interact directly with the breeding box  
- Watch real-time beanworm reactions  
- Influence their movement and behavior  

Every interaction provides immediate visual and gameplay feedback.

---

## 🌿 Why Bean Worm?

- Easy to learn, relaxing to play  
- Focused on interaction rather than pressure  
- A soothing mix of **feeding, collecting, and creature care**  
- Ideal for short play sessions with a sense of progression
